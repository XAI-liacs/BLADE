\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\pages{0.25}

\plan{The generator knows more than it is asked: whether its offspring
will crash, the fitness of the parent, the algorithmic concepts it writes.
Reading this state saves evaluations for free; writing it gives a
behaviour dial that transfers across problems, but the evolutionary loop
needs to be designed to keep what the dial produces. Future work:
steering the selection, larger models, multi-objective dial control.}
