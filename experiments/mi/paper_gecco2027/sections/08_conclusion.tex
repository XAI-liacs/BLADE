\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The generator of an LLM-driven algorithm design loop knows more than it
is asked: whether its offspring will crash, the fitness of the parent,
and the algorithmic concepts it writes. Reading this state
reveals failures before any code runs, also on new problems, although
on cheap problems a smoke test is the better filter. Writing it gives a dial that
transfers across problems and changes which algorithms the model
designs. In the loop, the gain comes from when the dial is turned
(adapted to stagnation, never at initialisation), not from a fixed
setting per landscape. Future work includes dose control with bandits,
steering the selection of parents as well as their variation, and
larger models.
