\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\pages{0.25}

\plan{The generator knows more than it is asked: whether its offspring
will crash, the fitness of the parent, the algorithmic concepts it
writes. Reading this state saves evaluations for free, also on new
problems. Writing it gives a dial that transfers across problems and
changes which algorithms the model designs; in the loop, the gain comes
from when the dial is turned (adaptive to stagnation, never at
initialisation), not from a fixed setting per landscape. Future work:
dose control with bandits, steering the selection, larger models.}
