\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The LLM operator of an algorithm design loop need not be a black box.
Opened with mechanistic interpretability, it knows more than it is
asked: whether its offspring will crash, the fitness of the parent, and
the algorithmic concepts it writes. It listens selectively: to the
parent code and the error trace, not to the score. And it can be given
a parameter: a direction read from its own state, with a dose that
transfers across problems, changes which algorithms the model designs,
and in the loop works best when adapted to the success of the
offspring, like the step size of an evolution strategy. EC has the
methods to control such parameters; future work includes dose control
with bandits, steering the selection of parents as well as their
variation, stopping doomed generations early from the model's state,
and larger models.
